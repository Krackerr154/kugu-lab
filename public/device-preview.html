KUGU Lab Device Preview

Accurate here: real viewport width, so every @media query and Tailwind breakpoint (sm:640, md:768, lg:1024) fires exactly as on a real device. Horizontal overflow, uncollapsed grids and clipped text are genuine.

NOT emulated: touch events, hover vs :active on touch devices, device pixel ratio (font sharpness), mobile user-agent, iOS safe-area insets. For those use Chrome DevTools device toolbar (Ctrl+Shift+M) or a real phone on your LAN.
